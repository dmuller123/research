Daniel S. Müller

Daniel received his diploma in chemistry in 2008 under the guidance ofProf. Bernhard Breit at the University of Freiburg, Germany. In 2012, he earned his Ph.D. in chemistry from the University of Geneva under the direction of Prof. Alexandre Alexakis. As a SNSF fellow, Daniel conducted postdoctoral studies with Prof. Larry E. Overman at the University of California, Irvine (USA). In 2014, Daniel joined Prof. Ilan Marek's group at the Technion (Haifa, Israel). In 2016, he moved to Rennes (France) and conducted postdoctoral research with Dr. Marc Mauduit at the École Nationale Supérieure de Chimie de Rennes (ENSCR) until 2019. In 2020, he was appointed CNRS researcher at the Institute of Chemical Research in Rennes (France).
